# Menu audio

Sounds are wired in `MenuBootstrap` (composition root) and driven by the same pointer-down hook as the wizard cast (see `RENDERING.md`).

| Event | Trigger | Sound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hover | `PointerEnterEvent` (trickle-down on the panel root) whose target is an enabled `Button` | `hover` |
| Press | `PointerDownEvent` / `NavigationSubmitEvent` on an enabled `Button` | `click`, or `back` for `back-button` |
| Cast | Every wizard `Cast()` (`SpellSoundWizardAnimator` decorates `IWizardAnimator`) | random `spellClips` entry, never the same twice in a row |

Hover only counts the `Button` itself: its children raise their own enter events while the pointer is already inside, so they are ignored.

## Swapping sounds

Edit `Assets/_Project/Audio/MenuAudioLibrary.asset` in the Inspector: clip and volume per slot, plus the spell clip list and volume. No code change needed. Any number of spell clips works.

Import settings used: UI `.ogg` mono, Decompress On Load, Vorbis 70%; spell `.wav` mono, Compressed In Memory, Vorbis 70%.

## Scene

- `MenuUI` has the UI `AudioSource` (2D).
- The wizard root (the object with the `Animator`, not its FBX child of the same name) has the spell `AudioSource` (2D).
- `MenuCamera` keeps the `AudioListener`.

## Music

Two looping layers play together under the `MenuMusic` object (`Theme` and `Ambient` `AudioSource`s, 2D, loop, play on awake off):

| Layer | Clip | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Theme | `Audio/Music/MenuTheme.wav` | Original chip-style loop (D Phrygian dominant, 84 BPM, 91.4 s) generated by `art/audio/compose_menu_theme.py` |
| Ambient | `ThirdParty/OpenGameArt/CathedralInTheForest/cathedral_forest.ogg` | CC0, see `ASSET_CREDITS.md` |

Both clips are imported as Streaming, Vorbis quality 0.6 (the theme is mono, the ambient track stereo). `MenuAudioLibrary` holds `themeVolume` (0.55), `ambientVolume` (0.45) and `musicFadeInSeconds` (2.5). `MenuMusicPlayer` (pure, driven by `Tick`) fades both layers from silence to their targets; `UnityMusicLayers` starts them with `PlayScheduled` on a shared dsp time so they stay aligned. `MenuBootstrap` wires it and does nothing if the library or either source is missing.

Regenerate the theme with `python art/audio/compose_menu_theme.py <out.wav>` and replace `MenuTheme.wav`.

## Menu sound mixer

A `SOUND` button at the top right of every menu screen opens a popover with `MUTE ALL` and two channels. The overlay lives in `ScreenLayout.uxml` (above the screen content), so it is mounted once and shown on Title, Login, Register, Verify, Guest and Lobby. Pattern: `SoundSettings` (pure model) + `SoundMixerPresenter` + `ISoundMixerView`/`SoundMixerView`, composed in `MenuBootstrap`.

| Channel | Covers | Applied by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Music | Theme and ambient layers | `MenuMusicPlayer` multiplies each layer target by the music multiplier on every tick, so changes are live and land correctly mid fade-in |
| Effects | UI hover/click/back and spell one-shots | `UnityMenuSfx` and `UnitySpellSfx` multiply the one-shot volume by the effects multiplier |

Rules:

- Levels are 0-100 in steps of 5 and act as multipliers on the existing mix; the default (100%, nothing muted) sounds exactly as before.
- Moving a channel slider sets its level and unmutes that channel.
- A channel shows `Off` (and its slider is dimmed) when it or the master is muted. Master mute never changes channel levels or mute flags.
- The trigger shows the crossed speaker when the master is muted or both channels are muted.
- Pressing the trigger again, Escape or a navigation cancel closes the panel.
- Everything under the overlay carries the `hf-no-cast` class: its controls keep click/hover sounds but never make the wizard cast.

Persistence: `PlayerPrefsSoundSettingsStore` (`ISoundSettingsStore`) saves on every change under `hexfall.sound.master_muted`, `music_level`, `music_muted`, `effects_level` and `effects_muted`. On WebGL PlayerPrefs is backed by IndexedDB. Loaded values are clamped and snapped to the step by `SoundSettings`.

Not created yet: `AudioMixerController` is internal and cannot be built from scripts. Create `Audio/HexFallMixer.mixer` in the editor (Assets > Create > Audio > Audio Mixer), add `Music` and `SFX` groups under `Master`, expose `MusicVolume` and `SfxVolume`, and set each `AudioSource`'s Output (UI and spell sources to `SFX`, music sources to `Music`).

## Web

Browsers block audio until the first user gesture, so hover sounds before the first click are silent in WebGL. On WebGL the music starts on the first pointer down, key press or submit and fades in from that moment; in the editor and desktop builds it starts immediately.

## Player surface sounds

The wizard's contact sounds come from the surface under it (`PlayerSurfaceSensor`, textures mapped to `SurfaceDefinition` assets by `SurfaceRegistry`). Each surface lists effects per impact type (`Footstep`, `Landing`, `Slide`) with clips, a volume range and a pitch range.

| Impact | Component | Trigger |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Footstep | `PlayerFootsteps` | Foot contacts while walking or running |
| Landing | `PlayerLandings` | Grounded after a fall or jump; louder with the impact speed |
| Slide | `PlayerSlideScrape` | Entering the Slide locomotion state |

Slide scrape: one clip, `ThirdParty/OpenGameArt/Scrapes/SlideScrape.wav` (CC0 "Scrapes" by AntumDeluge, derived with `python -I art/audio/derive_slide_scrape.py <scrape-1.wav> <SlideScrape.wav>`: -8 dB, 10 ms fade-in, 150 ms fade-out), shared by every surface and tuned per surface through the Slide effect's volume and pitch ranges (grass and wood quieter and lower, metal higher). `PlayerSlideScrape` plays it once on its own 2D `AudioSource` when the slide starts: volume = Slide volume range lerped by the entry planar speed (`fullSpeed`, 6 m/s) x `scrapeVolume` x the Effects level. When the slide ends it fades out over `fadeOutSeconds` (0.12 s) and stops. A surface without a Slide effect stays silent. The pure maths (`SlideScrapeLevels`) is covered by `SlideScrapeTests`.

Surfaces and their sounds: Stone and Brick (concrete steps, heavy soft landings), Grass (grass steps, medium soft landings), Wood (wood steps, light wood landings), Metal (light metal steps, heavy metal landings), Carpet (carpet steps, medium soft landings, a quieter and lower-pitched slide scrape). Default has only the slide scrape. The Sandbox has a 4 x 4 m test patch for Wood, Grass, Metal and Carpet.
